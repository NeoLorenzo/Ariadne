# Ariadne App Goal

## Purpose

Ariadne is a low-friction personal operating workspace for deciding what matters, turning
that direction into projects and tasks, and seeing whether real work is moving outcomes forward.

## Core product goal

Make daily execution visibly connected to longer-term strategy:

1. Set one active direction.
2. Define a small number of strategic objectives.
3. Attach measurable outcome goals.
4. Organize projects and granular tasks around those outcomes.
5. Surface deadlines, stalled work, and external progress signals automatically.
6. Review progress without maintaining a separate reporting system.

## Non-negotiables

- Fast startup and low interaction cost
- Local-first behavior with safe cloud synchronization
- Clear ownership from direction to objective to goal
- Useful prioritization without administrative busywork
- Private-by-default user data
- Desktop and mobile usability

## Success criteria

- The dashboard explains current priorities at a glance.
- Projects and tasks stay actionable instead of becoming passive lists.
- Important changes retain enough history to explain why strategy shifted.
- External signals expose neglected areas without manual reporting.
- The app remains dependable when cloud services are unavailable.
